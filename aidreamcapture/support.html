Common Questions

How do I record a dream?

Tap the microphone button and describe your dream using your voice. The app will transcribe your words and use AI to generate a personalized story.

How do I manage my subscription?

Go to Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions on your device to manage or cancel your AI Dream Capture Pro subscription.

How do I request a refund?

All purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com or contact Apple Support directly.

Where are my stories stored?

All stories and audio narrations are stored locally on your device. We do not store your content on any external servers.
